GamePulse - Google Play listing art
====================================

Where each file goes in Play Console
(Grow users > Store presence > Main store listing > Graphics)

  App icon ............ app-icon-512.png              (512 x 512)
  Feature graphic ..... feature-graphic-1024x500.png  (1024 x 500)
  Phone screenshots ... phone\01..06                  (1080 x 1920, upload all 6 in order)
  7-inch tablet ....... tablet-7in\01..06             (1200 x 1920)
  10-inch tablet ...... tablet-10in\01..06            (1600 x 2560)

Order tells the story: 01 Feel every Moment, 02 Cheer the GameMakers, 03 Find what's hot,
04 Post your skills in seconds, 05 Club Wars, 06 Live alerts.
Play needs at least 2 phone screenshots; tablet ones are optional but make the app show up
better on tablets and Chromebooks.

The phone screens are drawn illustrations of the app (sample creators and numbers). Before the
public launch, swap in 1-2 real screenshots once there's real content.

To change text or re-render
  Claude outputs\ad-video-source\store-screenshots.mjs  (headlines are at the top, SHOTS)
  In that folder:  node store-screenshots.mjs
