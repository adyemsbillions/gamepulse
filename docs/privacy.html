What is public

Your username, display name, profile photo, country, favourite club, bio, Moments, comments, Pulse rank, and Fan and Supporting counts can be seen by anyone using GamePulse. Saves, blocks, mutes, "Not interested" choices, reports, saved stickers and notifications are private to you. Videos you download from GamePulse carry the GamePulse logo and the creator's username.

Legal bases

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (contract), to keep GamePulse safe and working (legitimate interests), and where the law requires it. Push notifications use your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. If you delete a Moment, we remove it and its video. If you delete your account, we delete your profile, Moments, comments and other activity within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer. Server logs are kept for up to 90 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. Email [CONTACT EMAIL]; we reply within 30 days. You can delete your account at any time: see Delete your account. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or, in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

GamePulse is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian. If we learn we hold data from a child under 13, we delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access rules in our database limit who can read and change each kind of data. No service is perfectly secure, so please protect your Google account with a strong password and two-step verification.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
