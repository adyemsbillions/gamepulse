Delete your GamePulse account

GamePulse, by Cravii Food and Logistics Ltd

You can delete your GamePulse account and its data at any time, either in the app or by emailing us.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open GamePulse and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab.
  3. Tap the settings icon (top right).
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Read what will be deleted, tap Delete my account, then Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

No longer have the app?

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my GamePulse account" and your GamePulse username. We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, username and photos
  • Every Moment you posted, and its video
  • Your comments, stickers, Cheers, replays and saves
  • Who you support and who supports you
  • Your Pulse points, streaks and challenge entries
  • Your notifications, push token, blocks and mutes

What may be kept

  • Server logs, kept for up to 90 days for security, then deleted.
  • Reports about content that broke our rules, and records we must keep by law, for as long as the law requires.
  • Copies other people already saved or shared outside GamePulse, which we can't reach.

Deleting your account can't be undone. Your Google account itself is not affected.
